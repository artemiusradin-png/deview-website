# Private enterprise AI content direction and 40 keyword candidates
23 October 2026. These are proposed targets; no volume, difficulty or ranking figures have been measured. The accompanying CSV contains exactly 40 unique terms.

## Positioning
Develop private enterprise LLM development as a recognizable offer alongside custom software and enterprise platforms. A useful positioning line is: “Private enterprise AI, built around your data and workflows.”

Describe four connected delivery areas:
1. Private assistants and enterprise search: retrieval over company documents, source citations, identity and permission controls.
2. Model adaptation: task evaluation, data preparation and fine-tuning where it improves the agreed task.
3. Deployment and operation: on-premise or private-cloud model serving, access controls, monitoring, cost and performance management.
4. Applications and agents: integration with existing business systems, workflow actions and human review.

State the actual deployment boundary clearly. Private networking, model hosting, data handling and access controls are separate design decisions. Scope each offer to capabilities the team can demonstrate.

## Page strategy
Strengthen /en/solutions/private-llm-deployment as the deployment destination, and /en/services/ai-development for the wider development offer. A distinct enterprise RAG page could explain document ingestion, permissions, retrieval quality, citations and integration. A dedicated fine-tuning page should follow concrete delivery evidence and a clearly different search intent.

The keyword CSV proposes two new destinations; no new landing pages have been implemented or published. Related terms share pages. Forty keywords do not imply forty landing pages.

Keep broader custom software, enterprise-platform, lending and industrial work visible. The private-AI group is a focused growth opportunity, not a completed positioning change.

A practical first batch is the deployment comparison, RAG-versus-fine-tuning guide, and an expanded project case with genuine measurements. The cost article needs supported assumptions before publication.
